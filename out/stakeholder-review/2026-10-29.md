# Stakeholder Acceptance Review: Ecommerce Customer Support Agent

- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Build:** branch `feat/issue-41-e2e-smoke` @ `272c51d`
- **Reviewer role:** Project Owner, final acceptance before client delivery
- **How tested:** UI only (Streamlit at http://localhost:8501, API at :8080, `/health` returned OK). About 90 messages across 6 personas, desktop and 375px mobile. Server logs were read only to explain errors seen in the UI.
- **Evidence screenshots:** `out/stakeholder-review/2026-10-29-img/`
- **Side effects of testing:** two test tickets were written to the store DB (`SupportTicket` id 5 = John Doe, id 6 = "Lan", `lan@gmail.com`, phone `12ab`). Remove them before the demo.

---

## 1. Verdict: **NOT READY**

The look and feel, the policy Q&A, privacy/prompt-injection defence and the ticket approval card are good. But a normal shopper reaches a dead end within a few messages. (1) **Conversations break for good.** If the user sends a message while the bot is still replying, or types instead of clicking Yes/No on an approval card, the thread can fail permanently. Every later message shows "Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again in a moment", and trying again never helps. (2) **Product search, the core feature, is unreliable.** Products that exist are reported as "not in our store" for partial names, typos, colour questions, and even the User Guide example `Give me some products in category "jacket"` (2/2). The customer is then pushed to Google Shopping, where competitor items are shown as if they were ours. (3) **The bot sometimes states wrong policy as fact.** Examples: free shipping on a 500,000 VND order, and lifetime warranty "only for bags and leather" (the policy says the reverse). (4) **Promised features fail:** history is lost on refresh, and the Google Shopping offer never appears for products we really don't sell (iPhone, sneakers). Each of these alone would embarrass us in a client demo.

## 4. Improvements (not bugs)

- **Currency:** product prices are in USD ($19–$59) while shipping is "free over 1 million VND". Customers can't tell whether an order qualifies. Show both, or pick one.
- **Product data:** there is no colour field, yet product descriptions mention colours. Every product lists sizes "S,M,L,XL,XXL" (one shows "S,m,L"), and stock is per product, not per size. Customers will ask "is M in stock?".
- **Show alternatives:** when an item is out of stock or missing (e.g. "Long Sleeve Dress in brown", Polo Sweater), suggest similar in-stock items from our store before offering Google.
- **Ticket UX:** show a ticket number. Let the customer look up their own ticket status. Pre-fill the subject from the description. Warn before creating a duplicate ticket (John Doe's ticket was saved 4 times across test runs).
- **Mobile:** "New chat" and "Share" are hidden behind the collapsed sidebar. Add them to the header.
- **Speed:** stream a "Searching our catalogue…" status for anything over 3 s, and cache category and policy lookups.
- **About Us:** remove the accessories, lingerie and gadget imagery the store doesn't sell, and use the real store name.
- **Share:** make shared links read-only, expire them, and strip saved personal facts.
- **Answer directness:** for "Can I pay with Momo/PayPal?" or "Is my 800k order free?", start with a clear Yes/No.
- **Client-requested features likely next:** order tracking by order number, size guide, human hand-off or live-agent escalation, opening-hours aware replies, and an analytics view of top questions and failed searches.

## 5. Top 5 must-fix items before delivery

### 1. Chat thread breaks permanently after sending during a reply or ignoring a Yes/No card
**Problem:** If a customer sends a message while the bot is still replying, or types a new message while a Yes/No approval card is showing, the conversation can break for good. Every later message shows "Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again in a moment." Retrying never helps; only "New chat" does. In other runs, messages are silently lost.
**Steps:** New chat → send `Give me product "Daily Metagent T-shirt"` → within 2 s send `Is it in stock?` → send `hello`. Or: trigger an approval card → type `never mind. How can I cancel my order?` instead of clicking.
**Server log:** `openai.BadRequestError 400: An assistant message with 'tool_calls' must be followed by tool messages responding to each 'tool_call_id'`.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Chat input is disabled, or messages are queued, while a reply is streaming.
- A new message while a card is pending is treated as "No" or cancel, then answered.
- A thread with an orphaned tool call recovers automatically (no permanent 400).
- No user message or bot answer is lost.
- Automated test covers both paths.

### 2. Product search says in-stock products are "not in our store"
**Problem:** Partial names, typos, colour questions and category browsing often fail and push the customer to Google Shopping. This includes the User Guide example.
**Steps (fresh chat each):** `Give me some products in category "jacket".` (11 in DB); `do you have a velvet dress?`; `What sizes and colors does the Eclipse Flared Lace Dress come in?`; `Do you have the Savage Lose Blazr?`. After one miss, later exact-name lookups in the same thread also fail.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Category browse returns store products for every category in the DB.
- Case-insensitive partial and fuzzy name match.
- Unknown attributes (colour) are answered as "not listed" instead of "product not found".
- Google offer only after the store search really returns 0 rows.
- An eval set of 20+ name variants passes ≥95%.

### 3. Google Shopping flow: wrong trigger, misleading results, false claims
**Problem:** The offer never appears for real non-store items (iPhone 15, Nike sneakers: 3/3). It appears for our own products instead. Results are shown as our product without a retailer name, with a broken raw link (spaces in the URL). After "No", the bot says it searched Google.
**Steps:** `Do you sell iPhone 15?` → no offer. `Do you have the Savage Lose Blazr?` → Yes → competitor blazer at $79.90, raw markdown link. Any miss → No → "couldn't find… or via Google Shopping".
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Non-store items always get the Yes/No offer.
- Results are labelled "From other retailers", with the retailer name and working, URL-encoded links.
- The "No" reply does not claim a search happened.
- The store name is never garbled ("IIVIVI").

### 4. Bot states incorrect store policy (free shipping, warranty, account changes)
**Problem:** Answers are sometimes produced without checking the policy documents, and contradict them.
**Steps:** `How much is shipping for a 500,000 VND order…` → "shipping is free!" (policy: over 1M VND only). `Do my jeans and my leather jacket get lifetime warranty?` → "lifetime only for bags and leather" (policy: bags and jeans excluded, leather 6 months). `How do I change my email?` → hotline (FAQ: email CSKH@ivy.com.vn).
**Also:** fix conflicts in the source documents (delivery 7 days vs 3–5 business days; refund 10–15 days vs max 30 days) and the FAQ category list (lists Shorts, omits Skirts).
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Policy questions always use the document lookup.
- Promptfoo/LangSmith eval with these questions and the ground-truth answers passes 100%.
- Source documents are internally consistent.

### 5. Conversation lost on page refresh, and shared links expose customer data
**Problem:** Refresh starts an empty chat unless "Share" was clicked first (thread id lives only in the Streamlit session). Separately, anyone with a Share link can continue the owner's chat and see their saved facts ("Here's what I've saved about you, Mai: Size M"). Tickets in the thread would expose email and phone.
**Steps:** Chat → refresh → history gone. Chat → Share → open the link in a new tab → `What do you remember about me?`.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Refresh restores the same conversation (thread id persisted in the URL or a cookie).
- Shared links open read-only, with a "Continue in a new chat" button.
- Shared view does not reveal saved user facts or ticket contact details.
- E2E test covers refresh and share.
